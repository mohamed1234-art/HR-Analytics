# 🔥 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a comprehensive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze employee data, identify attrition patterns, and uncover the real drivers behind employee turnover.

The dashboard transforms HR data into actionable insights that help organizations improve:

* Employee retention
* Workforce planning
* Employee engagement
* Performance management
* Work-life balance strategies

The analysis focuses on understanding **why employees leave**, not just how many leave.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze employee attrition patterns
* Identify high-risk employee groups
* Measure the impact of salary, overtime, and training
* Evaluate work-life balance factors
* Support HR decision-making using data-driven insights

---

# 📊 Key Insights from the Analysis

## 🚨 1️⃣ Attrition is Highly Concentrated

Attrition is not a company-wide issue — it is concentrated within specific employee groups.

### High-Risk Groups

* Sales Representatives → **40% Attrition**
* Fresh Graduates → **44.4% Attrition**

### Business Insight

The organization should focus retention efforts on specific high-risk segments instead of applying general solutions across all departments.

---

## 💰 2️⃣ Salary is NOT the Main Retention Driver

Even with:

* High salary increases
* Competitive compensation packages

Attrition rates remain high.

### Business Insight

Compensation alone does not guarantee employee retention.

Employee experience has a greater impact than salary.

---

## ⚠️ 3️⃣ Non-Financial Factors Drive Attrition

### Key Drivers

* Overtime → **24.68%**
* Frequent Business Travel → **25.19%**
* Poor Work-Life Balance → **32%**
* Low Job Involvement → **32.9%**

### Business Insight

Stress, burnout, and poor work-life balance are major contributors to employee turnover.

---

## 📉 4️⃣ Surprising Finding

### Insight

High Salary Increase ≠ Lower Attrition

In several cases, employees receiving strong salary raises still left the company.

### Business Insight

Retention problems are more connected to:

* Employee satisfaction
* Engagement
* Career development
* Workplace experience

than financial compensation.

---

## 📚 5️⃣ Training Significantly Improves Retention

### Findings

* High Training Participation → Attrition drops to **9.6%**
* Low Training Participation → Attrition rises to **18%**

### Business Insight

Training and career development are among the strongest employee retention factors.

---

# 🎯 Actionable Business Recommendations

## 1️⃣ Focus on Fresh Graduates

### Recommendations

* Build structured onboarding programs (3–6 months)
* Assign mentors for new employees
* Define clear career paths
* Conduct regular follow-ups during the first year

---

## 2️⃣ Improve High-Risk Roles (Sales Team)

### Recommendations

* Redesign commission structures
* Reduce unrealistic performance pressure
* Improve leadership support
* Create clear internal promotion opportunities

---

## 3️⃣ Control Overtime & Workload

### Recommendations

* Redistribute workload fairly
* Establish overtime policies
* Hire additional staff when necessary
* Continuously monitor workload levels

---

## 4️⃣ Improve Work-Life Balance

### Recommendations

* Introduce hybrid/flexible work models
* Respect off-hours communication boundaries
* Promote employee wellness programs
* Encourage employees to use vacation leave

---

## 5️⃣ Invest in Training & Development

### Recommendations

* Create annual training plans
* Connect training with promotions
* Develop technical & soft skills
* Build a continuous learning culture

# 💡 Key Business Takeaway

Employee retention is not only about salary.

The strongest drivers of attrition are:

* Poor employee experience
* High workload
* Weak engagement
* Lack of growth opportunities
* Poor work-life balance

---

# 🚀 Final Conclusion

HR Analytics proves one important fact:

**People don’t leave companies — they leave experiences.**

By leveraging data analytics, organizations can better understand employee needs, improve workplace culture, and create long-term retention strategies driven by real insights instead of assumptions.
